How to Play
🎯 The Idea
A question-and-answer game where correct teams earn the right to throw a dart — blindly — at the board. Points depend on where they land. But some areas trigger silly actions instead!
📋 Round Flow
1
Show the question. The teacher displays it on screen. Start the timer if needed.
2
All teams answer simultaneously on paper or mini-boards. No shouting out.
3
Reveal the answer. Mark which teams got it right using the check buttons.
4
Throw time! One member from each correct team comes up. The teacher clicks Reveal & Throw — scores appear on the board.
5
Eyes closed! The student closes their eyes and touches the screen anywhere. Points are awarded for the spot they hit on the dartboard.
6
Action zones! If they touch the coloured background instead of the dart, they get 0 points but must perform that action in front of the class.
🧩 Dart Modes
🎯 Vanilla — Classic concentric rings. The centre is always worth the most (50 pts). Outer rings score less.
🧩 Adventurous — The board is divided into irregular colour patches, each worth a different score. The shapes shift after every throw, but all teams face the same set of scores in one round.
💡 Tips
• Teams that didn't answer correctly simply skip their throw that round.
• You can type questions manually during the game — you don't need a CSV.
• Use the leaderboard button at the end of any round to end the game early.
• Action zones shuffle after every throw — nobody knows where JUMP! will be next.
